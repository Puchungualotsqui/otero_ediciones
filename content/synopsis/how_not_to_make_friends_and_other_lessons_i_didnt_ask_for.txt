At first glance, Daniel is just another teenager trying to stay invisible. He keeps the world at a distance, avoids close bonds, and prefers not to get involved. But everything begins to shift when a few unexpected people slip past his armor: Noodle, the most outcast boy in school, who stubbornly insists on being his friend; Zoe, a meddlesome girl with the soul of a storyteller, who believes in a strange legend about a teenage poet who helps the broken-hearted; and Gavin, Daniel’s childhood best friend, whose story quietly anchors the heart of this novel.
The plot moves between biting humor, unfiltered tenderness, and emotional grief. Almost without meaning to, Daniel begins a process of healing—letting friendship in, learning to share his pain, and allowing compassion to seep through the cracks. The legend of “the poet” becomes a pretext to ease other people’s suffering, but it soon becomes clear that Daniel is the one who needs help the most.
